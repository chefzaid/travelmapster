    Where have you been?

    TravelMapster is a playful travel atlas with a day ledger you can rely on. Color in the countries you've visited, record every trip with its dates, and always know how many days you have left in the Schengen area or before a tax-residency limit.

    👀 The map shows a sample traveler. Click a country, press Been there and watch it color in. Nothing is saved.

    Your travels, kept three ways

    🎨 The atlas

    Color in countries, pin the cities you loved, keep a wishlist and plan day-by-day itineraries. Collect stamps, badges and traveler ranks as the map fills up.

    📒 The day ledger

    Every visit has dates, so TravelMapster counts your days in each country: Schengen 90/180, your own visa limits and the 183-day tax rule, with alerts before a limit and a printable evidence report.
