Where have you been?

TravelMapster is a playful travel atlas with a day ledger you can rely on. Color in the countries you have visited, keep every trip with its dates, plan the next one, and always know how many days you have left in the Schengen area or before a tax-residency limit.

Free, no card needed. No ads, no trackers: your travels are yours.

Everything about your travels, in one map

🎨

The atlas

Click a country or a town and mark it been there, lived there, transit only or wishlist; the map colors itself in. Set your home country, choose cheerful, color-blind safe or print-friendly colors, and count against every territory on the map or the UN list.

📍

Places and visits

Every place keeps its visits with their dates, notes, photo links, ratings, tags and an emoji pin. Bring your past with you: import a Polarsteps export, a list of countries from Been or Visited, your photos or your phone's location history (read in your browser, reduced to towns), or a file from another app.

🕰️

Journeys through time

Slide the timeline to see your map as it was in any year, and replay your journeys as lines drawn from place to place. "On this day" brings back where you were in years past.

🗓️

Trips

Plan day-by-day itineraries to one place or several, filled with ideas from travel guides and shown as a route on the map. Keep a packing list and a budget in any currency, export the trip to your calendar, print it, and record it as visits once you are back.

🛂

Your passport

Stamps for every country, traveler ranks, badges, collections such as every EU member or every capital, and yearly goals. Share picture cards of your count, your stamps or your year, and download your map as a poster in standard print sizes.

📒

The day ledger

Because every visit has dates, TravelMapster counts your days in each country: the Schengen 90/180 rule, your own visa stay limits and the 183-day tax-residency threshold, with alerts before a limit, plans checked ahead of time and a printable evidence report for an adviser or a border officer.

🧮

Free Schengen calculator

Not ready for an account? Count your days in the Schengen area for any date, with past and planned stays. Nothing leaves your browser.

Open the Schengen calculator →

🔗

Share your map

Make your map public and share its link, with a preview picture when you post it; embed it on your blog; and choose whether search engines may list it. Notes, photos, your home country and where you are now stay private.

📦

Yours to keep

Works on any device and installs as an app that opens offline. Download all your data at any time, as JSON, CSV, GeoJSON or KML, and delete your account whenever you like.
